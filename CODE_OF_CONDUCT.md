# Code of Conduct

Örtü contributors are expected to create a welcoming, harassment-free environment. Be respectful, discuss ideas rather than people, assume good intent while addressing concrete impact, and accept constructive feedback.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, or sustained disruptive behavior are not tolerated. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, lock discussions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's private reporting channel. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with attention to the safety of the reporter.
